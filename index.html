          Timetables
          
        
        
          
          A train waits at each stop until its leaving time. Arrive sets when a driver is due. Times follow the clock,
          so choose Real on the clock for trains to keep time. Turn changes ends before leaving.

              Service diagram

                Controls
                
        
                
          
                
            
                Driving
                
            
                
              
                W, S
                
              
                More power; less power, then engine brake, then air brake
                
              
                Space
                
              
                Emergency brake while held
                
              
                P
                
              
                Autopilot on or off. Driving by hand scores your stops
                
              
                J
                
              
                Throw the next points ahead
                
              
                Q
                
              
                Acknowledge a cab safety warning or reset train protection
                
              
                R
                
              
                Change ends when stopped
                
              
                H
                
              
                Horn
                
              
                C
                
              
                Change camera (Chase, Cab, Trackside, Cinematic, Free); drag to look around
                
              
                Wheel
                
              
                Zoom camera, change field of view in Cab view, or move forward in Free view
                
              
                WASD, Arrows
                
              
                Fly around in Free view (hold Shift to move faster)
                
              
                N
                
              
                Drive the next train
                
              
                , and .
                
              
                Slow down or speed up the railway; the slowest is pause
                
              
                O
                
              
                Overview map, while driving or editing
                
              
                M
                
              
                Sound on or off. Use Sound in the menu to set levels
                
              
                E
                
              
                Switch between driving and editing
                
            
                
          
                
          
                
            
                Editing
                
            
                
              
                Right drag
                
              
                Rotate the view
                
              
                Middle drag, WASD
                
              
                Move the view
                
              
                Wheel
                
              
                Zoom
                
              
                Train tool
                
              
                Turn on Keep trains moving while editing and zoom in to hear passing trains
                
              
                [ and ]
                
              
                Lower or raise the track being laid
                
              
                Shift
                
              
                Snap a new line to 15° steps
                
              
                Esc
                
              
                Stop laying track
                
              
                Delete
                
              
                Remove the selected point (or drag a box with Remove to delete several items)
                
              
                Ctrl+Z
                
              
                Undo; Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z to redo
                
            
                
          
                
          
                
            
                Touch
                
            
                
              
                Desk
                
              
                Slide the lever for power and brake. Horn and Next train are on the desk
                
              
                One finger
                
              
                In the editor, rotate the view; with Select, drag the ground to move it
                
              
                Two fingers
                
              
                Move and zoom the editor view
                
              
                Tap twice
                
              
                Editor tools show what a tap will do first; tap the same place again to do it
                
              
                Menu, Trains
                
              
                On small screens the menu, clock controls and train list fold away behind these
